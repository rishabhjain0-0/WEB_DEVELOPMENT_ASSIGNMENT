Academic Terms & Definitions

Lecture
A formal presentation conducted by a faculty member to teach theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students conduct experiments and gain hands-on experience.
Tutorial
A small-group interactive session designed for problem-solving and clarification of doubts.
Internal Assessment
Continuous evaluation of a student's academic performance through tests, quizzes, and assignments.
Practical
An examination or session evaluating practical skills and applied knowledge in a subject.

Academic Rules

Classroom Rules

  • Maintain strict discipline and punctuality.
  • Cell phones must be switched off or kept on silent mode during lectures.
  • A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory to appear for end-semester examinations.
  • Keep the classrooms and department premises clean.

Practical Class Sequence

  1. Wear proper laboratory attire and safety gear.
  2. Review the lab manual and theoretical concepts beforehand.
  3. Sign the lab attendance register upon entry.
  4. Perform assigned experiments under faculty supervision.
  5. Clean the workstation and submit the lab report before leaving.
