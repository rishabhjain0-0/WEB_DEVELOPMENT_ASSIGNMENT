Admission Overview

Welcome to Global University. Our admission process is designed to select candidates based on academic excellence and holistic potential. Application Deadline: July 31, 2026.

"Our goal is to nurture future leaders through academic excellence, innovation, and character building. We welcome all prospective students to join our vibrant community."
— Office of Admissions

Eligibility

Applicants must meet basic academic criteria. Candidates must have completed higher secondary education with a minimum aggregate score of 60%.

Application Process

  1. Submit the online application form before the specified deadline.
  2. Upload all required academic and personal documents.
  3. Pay the non-refundable application fee.
  4. Appear for the university entrance examination or interview if applicable.
  5. Check the published merit list on the official portal.
  6. Attend the counseling session and confirm seat allotment.

Required Documents

  • High School (10th) Marksheet and Certificate
  • Higher Secondary (12th) Marksheet and Certificate
  • Government-issued ID Proof (Aadhaar / Passport)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate
  • Category or Reservation Certificate (if applicable)

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum qualification criteria required to apply for a program.
Merit List
A rank list of applicants generated based on academic scores or entrance test performance.
Counselling
The process where candidates choose their stream or course based on rank and seat availability.
Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to eligible students based on merit or need.
Registration
The final formal enrollment of a student into the university database after fee payment.
