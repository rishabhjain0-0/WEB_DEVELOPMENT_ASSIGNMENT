Destination Overview • Kyoto, Japan

Experience the perfect blend of timeless traditions and modern charm ™. Kyoto offers iconic historic temples, peaceful bamboo groves, vibrant seasonal festivals, and authentic culinary journeys ®.

Travel Tips & Visitor Information

  • Best time to visit: Spring (Cherry Blossoms)  →  March to May, or Autumn → October to November.
  • Local Currency: Japanese Yen (¥). Credit cards are accepted widely, but carrying cash is recommended for local markets.
  • Getting Around: Use the local subway network or purchase a daily bus pass for seamless travel.

One-Day Suggested Itinerary

  1. 08:00 AM – Check-in: Meet your tour leader at Kyoto Central Station.
  2. 09:30 AM – Guided Tour: Explore the Fushimi Inari Shrine gates.
  3. 12:30 PM – Local Lunch: Enjoy authentic Japanese Ramen at Nishiki Market.
  4. 03:00 PM – Scenic Walk: Stroll through the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove.
  5. 06:00 PM – Evening Evening Pass: Dinner and cultural Gisha performance in Gion.
